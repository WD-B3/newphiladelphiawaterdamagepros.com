When a ceiling shows water, the leak is usually above it and has been going for a while. Emergency ceiling water damage repair in New Philadelphia, OH covers the finished ceiling and whatever sits above it, including roof leaks, upstairs plumbing and attic HVAC. The 24/7 line answers when a ceiling starts to drip at night.

Stay out from under any bulge. Water-laden drywall can fall without warning. Put a bucket under a drip, move furniture and electronics away, and if a light fixture is wet, turn off that circuit only if it is safe to reach the panel.

When a ceiling leak is an emergency

A dripping ceiling, a sagging bulge or a stain that is growing in front of you is urgent. A bulge means water is pooled in the drywall and the weight is building. Puncturing it with a small hole over a bucket can relieve pressure, but only do that if the bulge is small and you can stay out from under the ceiling.

Call right away if the water is near a light, a fan or the panel, or if a wide area has changed color overnight. Tell the dispatcher what is above the room, such as an attic, a bathroom or a roof.

Lights and falling drywall

Never stand beneath a bulging ceiling. If water is flowing through a light fixture or smoke detector, stay out of the room and shut off the circuit only if it is safe. For any electrical risk, call the electric utility or 911 first.

Where the water comes from

Roof leaks

Missing or damaged shingles, flashing and vents let rain into decking and insulation.

Upstairs plumbing

Tubs, showers, toilets and supply lines can drip into the floor system.

HVAC in the attic

Condensate lines and drain pans can overflow onto the ceiling below.

Ice dams

Winter melt backs up under shingles and pushes water inside, which is a real risk in cold Ohio winters.

Plumbing sources overlap with our burst pipe water damage page, and condensate sources with our AC leak water damage page.

Roof leaks and what they do inside

Roofs do not leak in the same spot where the ceiling stains. Water runs along a rafter or decking before it drops. That means the stain may be several feet from the real entry point.

A roofer finds and repairs the roof. We handle what the water has already wetted.

Rain that comes through a roof wets insulation first. Wet insulation holds water against the drywall like a sponge. A short roof leak may leave a small stain.

A long one rots the decking, soaks the insulation and stains the ceiling in rings. Wind and storm damage to the roof itself is covered on our storm water damage cleanup page.

Signs the ceiling is failing

  • A yellow or brown stain, often with darker rings.
  • Sagging or a bulge in the ceiling surface.
  • Peeling paint and bubbling texture.
  • Drips during or after rain.
  • A damp or musty smell in the room or the attic.

How the repair works

Find the source

Trace the water upward and confirm the leak is stopped by you or a roofer or plumber.

Relieve and open

Release trapped water and cut away sagging or soaked drywall.

Dry the cavity

Remove wet insulation, then run air movers and dehumidifiers with moisture readings.

Hand off for rebuild

Drywall, texture and paint come after drying, as a separate scope.

Drying a ceiling is not just drying the visible stain. The joists, insulation and anything above also have to reach dry readings, which is covered on our structural drying and dehumidification page. If water ran down the walls, our wall and drywall water damage repair page applies too.

Repairing, not just painting

Painting over a stain with stain-blocking paint hides it, but it does not dry the wet material behind it. If the leak is still active or the cavity is wet, the stain returns. Verify dry readings first.

Ceiling water damage FAQ

Is a dripping ceiling something to call about right away, even at night?

Yes. A sagging ceiling can fall, so call at any hour and stay out from under it. A crew comes the same day when one is open.

Should I poke a hole in a bulging ceiling?

Only a small hole over a bucket, and only if you can stay out from under the ceiling. If the bulge is large or near electrical fixtures, do not touch it and call.

Do you repair roofs?

No. A roofer repairs the roof. We handle the water, drying and documentation inside.

Can a stained ceiling just be painted?

Not until the cavity is dry and the leak is stopped. Paint over wet drywall fails.

Does homeowners insurance cover a roof leak?

Often for sudden storm damage, less often for wear and neglect. Policy language varies, so ask your insurer how it handles this cause.
