Kitchens hold sink supply lines, a dishwasher, a refrigerator line, a disposal and often a garbage disposal drain, all in a few square feet. Kitchen water damage restoration in New Philadelphia, OH means finding which of those failed and drying the cabinets, floor and walls it wet. Our line is open 24/7 if water is spreading.

Shut off the valve under the sink, empty the cabinet and photograph the area before anything is moved. Lift anything absorbent off the floor, and keep the electric outlets dry.

Where kitchen leaks begin

Under-sink supply lines

Braided hoses and compression fittings loosen or corrode.

Dishwasher connections

The supply, the drain loop and the door seal can all leak.

Disposal and drain

A loose slip nut or a cracked trap drips into the cabinet base.

Faucet and sink seams

Failed caulk lets water run behind the countertop.

The appliance side of the story is covered on our appliance leak water damage page. This page focuses on cabinets, countertops and the kitchen floor.

Cabinets are the main question

Particleboard and MDF cabinet bases swell when they get wet, and swelling does not reverse. Solid wood and plywood bases handle moisture better but can still delaminate or stain. A cabinet that is wet for a short time can sometimes be dried with the doors off and air pushed behind the toe kick. One that has been wet for a long time or is badly swollen usually has to be replaced.

Countertops are less affected, but laminate tops can lift at the seams, and the sink cutout is a weak point. We tell you what stays and what goes before work starts, so you are not surprised by a demolition line on the estimate.

Signs of a hidden kitchen leak

  • A musty smell inside the cabinet under the sink.
  • Swollen toe kicks or cabinet side panels.
  • Warped or lifting flooring near the dishwasher.
  • Water stains on the ceiling below an upstairs kitchen.
  • A damp spot under the refrigerator.

How we dry a kitchen

Cabinets are opened, toe kicks are removed and small holes may be drilled in hidden cavities so air can move through them. Small air movers and a dehumidifier run while moisture meters track the readings. If vinyl or laminate flooring has been soaked, the edges are lifted to check the subfloor.

For the general drying stage, see our structural drying and dehumidification page. If the leak was a burst supply line, the burst pipe water damage page explains that scenario too.

Cabinets, toe kicks and the hidden floor

Kitchen floors usually run under the cabinets, and so does the water. Toe kicks, the recessed panels at the bottom of the cabinets, are often the first place swelling shows. Laminate and particleboard cabinet boxes swell and do not recover, while solid wood and plywood boxes dry better. A technician reads moisture at the toe kick, the cabinet base and the floor beside it before deciding what to pull.

Pulling a few toe kicks or drilling small access holes lets air reach the space behind the cabinets. That is often enough to dry the cavity without removing the cabinets. If the cabinets are glued or heavily swollen, removal may be needed, and we explain that before anything is cut.

Cabinet and floor drying follows the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard for water damage restoration, used as an industry guideline.

What to do before we arrive

  • Close the shutoff under the sink or behind the dishwasher.
  • Keep power off to wet outlets if the panel is safe to reach.
  • Empty wet lower cabinets and lift items off the floor.
  • Photograph the cabinets and the failed part.
  • Call the 24/7 line.

Kitchen water damage FAQ

Can swollen kitchen cabinets be saved?

Sometimes if they were wet briefly and are dried quickly. Swollen particleboard usually cannot be restored.

Should I remove my dishwasher after a leak?

Only after shutting off the supply and checking the floor. Heavy appliances can damage wet flooring.

How long does a kitchen take to dry?

Often several days. Enclosed cabinets and subfloor take longer than open rooms, so moisture readings decide.

What should I photograph?

The leak source, the wet cabinet, the floor, the ceiling below and any damaged belongings. Keep the failed part if you can.
