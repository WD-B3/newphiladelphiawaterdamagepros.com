Wall and Drywall Water Damage Repair in New Philadelphia, OH

Drywall is paper and gypsum. When it gets wet it softens, stains and grows mold, and the wall cavity behind it can stay wet long after the surface looks fine.

A wet wall rarely fails all at once. It stains, bubbles and softens, and the wet part is often behind the paint. Wall and drywall water damage repair in New Philadelphia, OH covers measuring the moisture, drying what can be dried and removing what cannot, so the wall can be rebuilt on a dry frame.

Water behind a wall can reach outlets and switches. If a wall is wet near electrical devices, turn off that circuit only if you can reach the panel safely and call an electrician if in doubt.

What wet drywall looks like

  • Brown or yellow stains that spread.
  • Bubbling or peeling paint and wallpaper.
  • Soft, crumbly or sagging drywall.
  • Swollen baseboards and trim.
  • Cracks along seams and a musty smell.
  • A cool, damp feel on the surface.

Stains can be old. Moisture meters tell whether the wall is wet now or only marked from an earlier leak.

Where the water comes from

Pipes in the wall

Supply and drain lines can leak inside a cavity for a long time.

Roof and ceiling leaks

Water runs down inside the walls from above, as covered on our ceiling water damage repair page.

Window and siding leaks

Rain finds gaps around frames and flashing.

Floor level water

Water on a floor wicks up the bottom of drywall.

Dry it or cut it out

Clean water that reaches drywall quickly can sometimes be dried in place. Drywall that is crumbling, sagging, moldy or soaked by contaminated water is cut out. Insulation behind it is usually removed, since wet insulation holds water.

Cutting a flood cut, usually about two feet up, also lets air reach the cavity. Wet framing is dried with air movers and dehumidifiers. Details are on our structural drying and dehumidification page.

How the repair works

Map the moisture

Meters and a thermal camera show where the wet area ends.

Open the wall

Remove wet drywall and insulation to expose the cavity.

Dry the frame

Run air movers and dehumidifiers until studs and plates read dry.

Hand off for rebuild

A contractor hangs new drywall, tapes, textures and paints.

Musty smell with no visible stain

If a wall smells musty but looks fine, moisture may be inside the cavity. A reading from a meter or thermal camera can confirm it. Do not seal it with paint or spray. If mold is visible, the cleanup is a separate scope, and our blog covers the smell question separately.

Walls wet by a sudden leak are often covered, and walls stained by slow seepage are less likely to be. Photograph stains, bubbles and baseboards before any opening is made.

Baseboards, trim and paint

Baseboards and trim are often the earliest visible sign of wall damage. Wood trim that has swollen and pulled from the wall can sometimes be removed, dried and reinstalled. Particleboard and MDF trim usually swells for good and has to be replaced. Paint that bubbles or peels tells you that moisture is moving through the wall, but it does not tell you how far.

Latex paint is not a moisture barrier. Painting over damp drywall traps the water, and the finish fails again, often within weeks. We confirm dry readings first, then the finish work can begin. Wallpaper behind which water has run usually has to come off for the wall to dry.

Deciding between drying and removing drywall follows the water categories in the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard.

What to do before we arrive

  • Stop the source if it is a known leak.
  • Stay away from outlets and switches in a wet wall.
  • Move furniture away from the wall.
  • Do not paint or seal a damp wall.
  • Call and describe the stain.

Wall and drywall water damage FAQ

Can wet drywall be dried and kept?

Sometimes, if the water was clean and caught quickly. Soft, sagging or moldy drywall is removed.

Can I just paint over a water stain?

Not until the wall is dry and the leak stopped. Paint over wet drywall peels and the stain returns.

How do you know there is water inside a wall?

Moisture meters and thermal cameras show wet areas without opening the wall.

Do you rebuild the wall?

No. We dry and remove. A contractor hangs and finishes new drywall.
