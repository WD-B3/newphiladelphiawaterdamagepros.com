Musty Smell After Water Damage in New Philadelphia, OH: Causes and Fixes

A musty smell after a leak means something is still damp. Finding that spot is the whole job, because air fresheners do not touch the cause.

By the New Philadelphia Water Damage Pros team · Updated November 9, 2026.

A musty smell is the sign of moisture in the wrong place. After a leak, it often means materials stayed wet, or that something got wet and nobody noticed. The smell comes from microbial growth and damp organic materials, and it will not leave until the moisture does.

This article explains the usual hiding places and how to find them. It is general information, and a moisture reading in your home is more reliable than any guess.

Where the smell comes from

Damp drywall, carpet pad, insulation, wood framing and subfloor all hold water. When they stay damp for days, organisms such as mold and bacteria feed on dust and paper on the surface. The smell is the by-product of that growth.

Humidity matters too. A house with high humidity can smell musty even when no one leak is to blame. A moisture reading helps tell the two apart.

The usual hiding places

Carpet pad

Pad holds water long after the carpet surface feels dry.

Wall cavities

Water that ran down inside a wall stays there. See wall and drywall water damage repair.

Under flooring

Hardwood, laminate and vinyl can trap water on the subfloor.

Crawl spaces and basements

Low spaces stay damp and feed odor upward. See crawl space water damage.

How to find it

Start with your nose and your eyes. The smell is strongest closest to the source, so walk the room and notice where it gets stronger. Look for stains, bubbles in paint, soft baseboards and warped flooring.

A moisture meter is better than guessing. Pushing a pin meter into drywall or a baseboard shows whether materials are wet. A thermal camera shows cool spots where evaporation is happening behind a surface. A pro can map the whole area in a short visit.

Why cleaning and fragrance do not work

Spraying or wiping covers the smell for a short time but leaves the moisture. Painting over a damp wall traps it. Running a household fan moves air but does not remove water from materials, and it can spread odor.

The sequence that works is to find the source, remove what cannot dry, dry the rest with equipment and confirm with readings. Then, if the smell remains, deodorization can be considered.

What drying and removal look like

Find and stop the moisture

Fix leaks and check for hidden sources.

Pad, insulation and soft drywall are removed.

Dry the structure

Air movers and dehumidifiers run until readings are normal.

When to call a specialist

If you see visible mold growth over a large area, or if anyone in the home has breathing problems that seem tied to the smell, consult a doctor and a mold specialist. We dry and clean water damage, and we do not test for or remediate mold.

Humidity control

Keep indoor humidity between about 30 and 50 percent when you can. A basic hygrometer is inexpensive and shows the number. Run a dehumidifier in damp basements, and make sure dryer vents and bathroom fans vent outdoors.

Smells that point to different sources

Not every damp smell means the same thing. A sour, sharp smell often comes from wet carpet or pad. An earthy, cellar-like smell points to damp soil, a wet crawl space or a basement wall. A rotten egg smell near a drain can point to a dry trap or a sewer gas problem, which is a plumbing issue.

A smell that appears only when the heat or air conditioning runs can come from the ducts, a drain pan or a wet insulation layer in the attic. A smell that gets worse after rain points to water entering from outside.

Tell the person who inspects your home when you notice it and what the weather and the house were doing. That context shortens the search considerably.

A quick home check you can do today

Place a hygrometer in the smelly room and another in a dry room for comparison. Record readings morning and evening for three days. A difference of more than ten points of humidity between rooms suggests a local moisture source in the damp one.

Look behind furniture pushed against exterior walls, inside closets on outside walls and under sinks. Pull back a corner of carpet near the wall if you can do so without damaging it, and feel the pad. Wet or cold pad is a clear answer.

The ANSI/IICRC S500 standard for water damage restoration calls for measuring moisture and drying to target, which is the step that ends most musty smells.

Humid summers and old basements

Summers in the Tuscarawas Valley can be humid, and many older homes have full basements that stay cool and damp. A cool basement meeting warm humid air produces condensation on walls and pipes, which can cause a musty smell even without a leak.

Check the FEMA Flood Map Service Center and your sump pump if the smell shows up after heavy rain.

Musty smell FAQ

How long should a musty smell last after cleanup?

If drying was complete, the smell should fade in days. A smell that lingers for weeks suggests something is still wet.

Is the smell dangerous?

It is a sign of moisture and possible mold. If you have health concerns, talk to a doctor.

Can a dehumidifier alone fix it?

It helps with humidity, but it will not dry a wet wall or floor. Find the source first.
