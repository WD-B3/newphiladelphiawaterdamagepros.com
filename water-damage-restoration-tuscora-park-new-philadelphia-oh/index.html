Safe first steps in a rainstorm

Do not go into a basement with standing water if there are outlets, a panel or appliances in the room. Do not drive or walk through water on a road. Report downed power lines to the utility and call 911 for any immediate danger.

When it is safe, photograph the water line and the damaged items, and lift small belongings off the floor. Keep fans and vacuums out of standing water. If the water came from a backed up drain or looks dirty, stay out and treat it as contaminated.

What to do before we arrive

Use this checklist while you wait.

Find where it entered

Check windows, wells, walls and drains from a dry spot, and note where water is coming in.

Keep electricity away from water

Stay out of the room if the floor is wet near outlets, and call the utility if the panel is wet.

Protect the dry side

Move furniture and boxes away from the wet edge, and take photos first.

Do not pump into the street

Wait for guidance before running pumps or hoses, since local rules and neighbors may be affected.
