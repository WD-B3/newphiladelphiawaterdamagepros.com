Wet Mattress or Sofa After a Leak in New Philadelphia, OH

Foam, fabric and padding soak up water and hold it. Whether a wet mattress or sofa can be saved depends on the water, the time and the materials.

By the New Philadelphia Water Damage Pros team · Updated November 9, 2026.

Call Now

Upholstered furniture and mattresses are built to absorb. That makes them hard to dry once they are soaked. The first question is what kind of water got on them, and the second is how long ago.

This article is a guide, not a guarantee. Some items can be saved. Some should not be.

The water decides

If the water was clean, such as a supply line or a tub overflow, many items can be dried if you act within a day or two. If the water was gray or black, such as sewage, river water or dirty overflow, porous items like mattresses and upholstered sofas should be discarded. They cannot be fully cleaned.

For contaminated water, see our sewage backup cleanup page, and for river water, our flood damage cleanup page.

Time matters

Clean water that sits on fabric and foam begins to cause odor and mold risk within a day or two. After that, drying is harder and the chance of saving the item falls. Act as soon as you can.

Mattresses

Often saved

  • Clean water, caught quickly.
  • A light surface wetting.
  • Mattress with a waterproof cover.

Often discarded

  • Gray or black water.
  • Soaked through to the core.
  • Water that sat for days with odor.

Foam and innerspring mattresses hold water deep inside. Even if the surface dries, the inner layers can stay damp. If you try to save one, extract what you can, stand it on edge in a dry, warm, ventilated room with a fan and a dehumidifier. If it still smells musty after several days, it should be discarded.

Sofas and upholstered chairs

Sofas have wood or metal frames, foam cushions, fabric covers and padding. The frame can often be dried, and the cushions may be saved if the water was clean. Remove cushions and covers, stand pieces up off the floor and air them in a ventilated space.

Leather and fabric can stain and shrink. A cleaner who works on upholstery may help after drying. Sofas that sat in contaminated water should be discarded.

Drying steps

Lift and separate

Get furniture off wet floors and remove cushions.

Blot, do not rub

Soak up what you can with clean towels.

Air out

Use fans and a dehumidifier in a ventilated room.

Check for smell

A musty smell after several days means moisture remains.

Do not use a hair dryer or heater at close range, and do not leave items in the sun if that could damage them. Do not place an item that is still wet back on a clean floor.

Document and decide

Photograph items before you throw them out and list their age and approximate value for your insurer. Renters insurance and homeowners policies often cover personal belongings after a covered loss. Ask your agent what your policy says before you rely on any coverage.

We handle the structure and flooring, and we can do contents drying in some cases. For furniture, a specialty cleaner or a replacement is often the practical answer.

The floor underneath

Wet furniture often means a wet carpet or floor under it. Pull furniture off the wet area and let the room dry too. Our carpet water damage restoration and structural drying pages describe what happens to the floor.

Getting rid of what cannot be saved

If a mattress or sofa must go, photograph it first and note its brand, age and approximate price. Wrap contaminated pieces in heavy plastic if you can, and ask your trash hauler or local waste office how to dispose of bulky items. Some haulers require an appointment or a fee for mattresses.

Do not leave wet furniture in a garage or on a porch for days, since it can grow mold and attract pests. Move it out of the house as soon as you can do so safely.

When you replace the item, check the room first. New furniture on a damp floor or against a damp wall will have the same problem. Wait until readings show the structure is dry.

Protecting the replacement

Once the room is dry, protect new furniture. Use a waterproof mattress cover, keep sofas a few inches off the wall in basements and use plastic risers under legs where the floor is prone to dampness.

The water categories in the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard are why porous items touched by contaminated water are discarded instead of cleaned.

Humid air and slow drying

Drying large soft items indoors depends on humidity. Summer in the Tuscarawas Valley can be humid, which slows drying, so a dehumidifier matters more than a fan. In winter, heated indoor air is dry, which helps.

If a leak came from a basement flood, be careful with items that touched river water, since river flooding is a contaminated water event. The March 1913 flood is a reminder of how much the valley can carry.

Wet furniture FAQ

Can a wet memory foam mattress be saved?

Memory foam holds water deeply and is hard to dry. If the water was clean and it was caught quickly, extraction and drying may work, otherwise replace it.

Will baking soda remove the smell?

It helps with surface odor, but it will not remove moisture. If the smell returns, the item is still damp.

How long can a sofa stay wet?

Try to start drying within a day. After two days, mold risk rises.
