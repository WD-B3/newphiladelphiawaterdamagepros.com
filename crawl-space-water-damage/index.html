A crawl space is a low, tight void under a floor, and when it gets wet the damage is out of sight. Crawl space water damage cleanup in New Philadelphia, OH covers standing water, soaked insulation, damp joists and the humidity that follows. The line is open 24/7 if water is rising, though many crawl space jobs are planned drying rather than emergencies.

Do not enter a crawl space with standing water if there is wiring, a heater or ductwork in it. Take photos from the access hatch and call.

Which homes have crawl spaces

Many homes here sit over a basement, and some sit over a crawl space or a slab. Crawl spaces are more common in certain house styles and additions. We do not claim a local share and have no figure to give.

If your house has one, the page below applies, and if it has a slab, see our slab leak water damage page. Basements are covered on our basement water damage cleanup page.

How crawl spaces get wet

Surface drainage

Roof runoff and poor grading push water toward the foundation vents and walls.

Plumbing leaks

Supply and drain lines run through the space, and a drip can go unnoticed for months.

Groundwater

High water tables and heavy rain can seep through the soil floor.

Humid air

Warm moist air meeting cool surfaces condenses on joists and ductwork.

What water does down there

Standing water wets the joists and the underside of the subfloor. Fiberglass insulation absorbs water, gets heavy and falls away from the floor above. Constant dampness raises the humidity inside the house and can warp hardwood flooring above, which is covered on our hardwood floor water damage repair page.

A vapor barrier on the soil floor is a plastic sheet that slows moisture rising from the ground. If it is torn, missing or pushed aside, the soil releases moisture all year.

Signs of crawl space trouble

  • Cupped or springy floors above the crawl space.
  • A musty smell that rises through floor registers.
  • Sagging or fallen insulation.
  • Condensation on ducts or pipes.
  • Wood that looks dark, soft or stained.

What we do

Inspect

Look at the space, the vapor barrier, insulation and any plumbing.

Remove water

Pump out standing water.

Remove wet insulation

Take out what cannot dry and bag it for disposal.

Dry and measure

Use air movers and dehumidifiers and track moisture content in the wood.

Wood framing has a moisture content, and a crawl space that stays damp will not reach safe levels on its own. We explain the process on our structural drying and dehumidification page. Tell us if a plumber has already fixed a leak, because then the cleanup can go faster.

Restoration versus prevention

Restoration

  • Removes standing water.
  • Dries joists and subfloor.
  • Documents moisture readings.

Prevention

  • Fixes grading, gutters and drainage.
  • Seals vents and adds a vapor barrier.
  • Adds a sump or dehumidifier where needed.

We do the first. A contractor does the second. A good restoration crew will tell you what it saw so you can fix the cause.

Crawl space FAQ

Is a wet crawl space an emergency?

Rising water or water near wiring is. A damp space with no active source is less urgent. The line is open 24/7 either way.

Do I have to go in myself?

No. Take photos from the hatch if you can and describe what you see.

Can wet insulation be saved?

Rarely. Fiberglass that has soaked usually has to be replaced.

Will drying stop the smell?

Often, but only if the moisture source is fixed. Otherwise the damp returns.

Do you encapsulate crawl spaces?

No. We restore after water damage. A foundation or crawl space contractor handles encapsulation.
