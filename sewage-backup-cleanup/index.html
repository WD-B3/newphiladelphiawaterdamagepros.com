When sewage comes up through a floor drain, tub or toilet, the water is contaminated and the room is a health hazard. Emergency sewage backup cleanup in New Philadelphia, OH covers removal of contaminated water, disposal of materials that cannot be cleaned, and sanitizing of what remains. The 24/7 line is open because backups tend to happen during heavy rain and at inconvenient hours.

Keep children, pets and anyone with a weak immune system out of the area. Do not run water, flush toilets or use the dishwasher or washer, because every drain in the house feeds the same line.

Is this sewage backup or a clogged drain?

Many people arrive here after a slow drain or a gurgling toilet, not a flood. A clog affects one fixture. A backup affects several, and water or waste comes up in the lowest drain in the house, often the basement floor drain or a ground floor tub.

These signs point to the main line rather than a single fixture.

  • Gurgling sounds from drains after running water elsewhere.
  • Several drains slow or blocked at once.
  • A sewer smell in the basement.
  • Dark water appearing in a floor drain or shower.

A plumber clears the line. We clean what the backup left behind.

When sewage is an emergency

Visible sewage on the floor, any amount of backup in living space or contamination near food, bedding or a child's play area counts as urgent. So does water that is still rising. Tell the dispatcher where the water is coming from, how far it has spread and what materials it has touched.

Do not clean this yourself

Sewage can carry bacteria and viruses. Do not touch it with bare hands, and do not use a shop vacuum on it. If you must go in, wear rubber boots, gloves and eye protection. If anyone has symptoms such as stomach illness after contact, call a doctor.

Why sewage backs up

Main line blockage

Roots, grease, wipes or a collapsed pipe stop the line.

Municipal surcharge

Heavy rain can overload the public system and push water back toward homes.

Failed sewage ejector

A basement pump that lifts waste to the main line can fail.

Cracked lateral

Old or damaged pipes let in soil and water.

Contamination also happens when flood water mixes with sewage, which is covered on our flood damage cleanup page. The water categories are explained for homeowners on our basement water damage cleanup page.

What Category 3 means

Water damage is sorted into three categories. Category 1 is clean water from a supply line. Category 2 is gray water from a washer or dishwasher.

Category 3 is grossly contaminated water from sewage or river flooding. Category 3 water means porous materials such as carpet, pad, drywall and insulation that touched it are usually removed, not dried. Hard surfaces can be cleaned and sanitized.

What we do

Contain

Seal the area and set up protection so contamination does not spread.

Remove

Extract contaminated water and take out porous materials that cannot be cleaned.

Clean and sanitize

Wash hard surfaces and apply an antimicrobial treatment.

Dry and verify

Run dehumidifiers and air movers until readings reach dry standards.

Odor is part of the job. After removal, deodorization and drying keep the smell from lingering in framing and subfloor. Our structural drying page explains how we confirm the result.

Belongings

Hard goods such as plastic, metal and sealed items can often be cleaned. Porous items such as upholstery, cardboard and mattresses usually cannot. Photograph them before disposal and keep a list for your insurer.

Sewage backup FAQ

Do you take sewage backup calls after hours?

Yes. A backup is a health matter, so the line is answered all day and all night. Keep people and pets away until we arrive.

Can I clean up a small sewage backup myself?

It is not recommended. Sewage carries bacteria, and porous materials often have to be removed. Call before you touch it.

Will the smell go away?

Yes, once contaminated materials are removed, the area is sanitized and the structure is dried.

Should I call a plumber first?

Call both. The plumber clears the line so the backup stops, and we handle the contamination. Stop using water in the house until the line is cleared.

Is sewer backup covered by insurance?

Often only with a sewer backup endorsement. Check your policy or ask your insurer.
