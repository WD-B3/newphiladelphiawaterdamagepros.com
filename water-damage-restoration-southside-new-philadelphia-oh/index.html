Southside is the residential area south of downtown New Philadelphia, built along South Broadway and the streets that branch from it. The name is used by real estate sites for streets with both SW and SE address suffixes, and no official boundary is published, so we describe it by landmarks. South Elementary at 132 Providence Avenue SW is one of the clearest ones. If water is coming into a Southside home right now, call (740) 231-9132.

What Southside houses look like

Recent sales listings tagged Southside describe many one story brick and vinyl sided ranch houses. Several of those listings mention finished lower levels, and a few mention an older Victorian home or a newer custom build. Street names that appear include South Broadway, 2nd Street SW, Briarwood Drive SE, Goshen Avenue SE and Colonial Avenue SE.

We do not have a count of basements in Southside, and not every house has one. Where a house does have a finished lower level, water is harder to spot, because carpet, trim and drywall can hide wet material for days. A damp smell in a finished basement is a reason to call, not to wait.

Heavy rain on April 2, 2024

Storms on Tuesday, April 2, 2024 brought heavy rain to Tuscarawas County. According to local radio, the city service department planned to pump rising water at the corner of Larkin Drive near South Elementary that evening. The story reported minor damage overall, so this was a drainage event, not a disaster, but it is a reminder that rain can collect on Southside streets.

Test your sump pump before the rainy season and keep gutters and downspouts clear. Point downspouts away from the foundation. These steps help but do not prevent every loss, and a pump can fail when the power goes out.

If a basement is taking on water

Do not walk into standing water if the basement holds a panel, an outlet or an appliance. Water and electricity can kill. Call the electric utility if you think the panel is wet, and keep children and pets upstairs.

Sewage is a separate case. Water from a floor drain, toilet or sewer line can carry bacteria, and household fans or a shop vacuum are not enough to deal with it. Stay out, keep the room closed off and call.

What to do before we arrive

These steps are safe for most homes.

Close the valve if you can

Shut the fixture valve or the main water shutoff only if it is dry and easy to reach.

Stay out of deep water

Do not wade into a flooded basement when electrical items are in the room.

Lift what you can

Move small items and rugs off wet floors and take pictures before they are moved.

Stop the pump trouble

If a sump pump has failed, tell the dispatcher, and do not run extension cords through water.

Call the line

Call (740) 231-9132 and give your street, the room and how deep the water is.

What a Southside job looks like

Call

A dispatcher takes your address and asks what the water touched.

Inspect

Extract

Standing water is pumped out and ruined material is removed.

Dry

Southside water damage FAQ

Do you cover every ZIP code in Southside?

Southside is part of New Philadelphia, ZIP 44663. Homes on streets with SE and SW suffixes are in the same city, and we confirm any edge case by phone.

Is emergency water removal available in Southside?

Yes. The emergency line is answered 24 hours a day, every day, including nights and holidays. We offer same-day response when available.

Is a damp finished basement an emergency?

Water that is still rising or coming from a pipe is. A damp wall with no active water can wait until morning, but do not leave wet carpet and drywall for days.

Does a sump pump stop basement water?

It helps, but pumps fail during power cuts and heavy rain. A battery backup can help and a regular test is cheap.

Is Southside in a FEMA flood zone?

Neighborhood-specific FEMA flood zone status is not verified here. Look up your own parcel on the FEMA Flood Map Service Center.
