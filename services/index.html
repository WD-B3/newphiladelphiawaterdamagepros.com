Every service below follows the same approach: stop more damage, remove the water, dry the structure and measure the result. What changes is the cause, the materials and the safety steps, which is why each service has its own page.

Water damage is urgent in most cases. If water is spreading right now, call (740) 231-9132. The line is open 24 hours a day, every day, and we offer same-day response when available.

How to choose a service page

Start with the cause if you know it. A split pipe, a failed water heater and a cracked appliance hose lead to different cleanup. If you only know what you see, such as a stained ceiling or a wet carpet, start with the place. And if you only know there is standing water, our water extraction page is the right first stop.

Some losses need more than one page. A burst pipe in a basement ceiling touches burst pipe water damage, ceiling repair and structural drying. We explain the combined scope on the phone.

Every service follows the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard for water damage restoration as its industry guideline.

What we do not do

We dry and clean water damage. We do not repair roofs, fix plumbing, waterproof foundations or rebuild finishes. Those are done by a roofer, plumber, waterproofing contractor and builder. We tell you what we found so those trades can fix the cause.
